### RTX 4090 broke

This wasn't fun, if I recall, I was doing a very long lasting job of setting each shopProduct up with it's links. I might have mentioned it before, but the idea was to get the best links for each ShopProduct and then instead of looking through the sitemaps each time, we've already got it done. WELL, that process can take a while and as a result, the top 4 rails wanted to melt. Now to be honest, I thought something smelt funny and I had a look, didn't see anything or smell anything, probably my lack of smell coming to bite me in the ass, but unfortunately, it broke. Boi me this caught me out.

I got the laptop setup, Docker and the images required with the .env and off to the racers. LM Studio with Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 @ Q8_0. This was painfully slow and forced me to change my CRON setup. It had to reduce a lot of the workload but it was required because it was now taking hours to get the biggest things done and don't get me started with looking over links.

For a while, I had looked at _Nscale_ but as I said previously, didn't want to use them. After lots of thinking, it became really apparent, unless I was saturating the RTX 4090 for nearly 8 hours a day at max capacity, this was going to cost me more, which was silly of me. As soon as I figured that out, I went onto nscale, started using their inference endpoint, and the rest is history. I spend about 7p a day and I'm pretty sure I can get that lower.

Basically while I could use the RTX 4090 all the time, well what's the point? Unless I want to continually check the websites all the time, thus get more use out of my GPU, that's a terrible way to look at things. Rather it's about how little can I get using the hardware I need without sacrificing the service. The changes I made still makes Cardsbolt fantastic to use, but it's extremely cheap to use. at 7p a day, I'm fairly sure just having the computer on with the RTX 4090, was far more than that! So that's that sorted.
